# Battle Rhapsody: place the rally

Valor can now decide where and when the party collects a chorus. Aim and spend
Harmony and Discord with Inspiration, Battle Hymn or Bolster. The cast places a
visible 95u rally 200u ahead for four seconds. Each living ally can collect
15% movement for three seconds by entering once. If Rallying Chorus is ready
at placement, each visitor can also collect its ordinary frontal block for
three seconds. The passive still has an eight-second recovery. A stronger
frontal guard remains in place. Bolster's chorus carries its movement cleanse.

The carrier's ordinary ability resolves at cast time. Inspiration still buffs
its usual recipient, Battle Hymn buffs the caster, and Bolster still provides
its self protection. The rally replaces the broad chorus delivery of the
extra movement, cleanse and passive block. Entering does not repeat the
carrier's direct ability effects. A new rally replaces the previous one
from the same owner. Downing or class change ends it.

At full HP, cast Dissonant Whispers, then Healing Word with Discord ready to
prepare Harmony. This does not award restored HP or trigger effective-heal
rewards. The ordinary effective-heal route and both verse timers still work.
All four first-pick builds can spend through Inspiration. Battle Hymn and
Bolster add alternative spenders. Blade Flourish and Shatter add Discord
routes. Additional riders on the old identity have been replaced by the rally.

In solo play, aim the rally along your next move, then cross into it before a
frontal attack. In a party, allies can collect it independently as they leave a
hazard or close on the boss. The field checks entry after player movement and
before projectile contact. Foes and hazards can cross it, and collecting its
effects does not require continued occupancy. This differs from Whispers'
decoys and a persistent aura that continually refreshes protection.

A bounded Sol 6.2 High review recommended shifting the chorus to a place
players can choose to enter. The retained Harmony or chorus component has a
rigid 10-value Forge premium for its respective behavior. The Forge identity
requires a retained chorus and access to both verses. Rallying Chorus
protection still requires the native passive.

Try a full-health opener with each first pick and place the rally on a retreat
route in co-op. Check whether its four-second pickup window is enough in a
busy fight. This patch also includes the requested [Faith follow-up](UNYIELDING_FAITH.md).
Next development is fixed-encounter whole-system comparisons, then ordinary
upgrade value, acquisition and grafts, and the visual milestone.
