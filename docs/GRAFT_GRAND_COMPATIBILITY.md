# Graft grand and identity compatibility

Forge can change the delivery and effects of the ability copied by a graft.
A grand now needs an active graft that can deliver the effect it improves.
The earn and equip checks use the resolved kit. Replay uses the same receiver
rules so old histories cannot apply a grand to a different payload.

| Graft             | Required receiving skill                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pyromancer's Pact | A damaging projectile with an impact blast                                                   |
| Shadow Pact       | Blink                                                                                        |
| Berserker's Howl  | A self-buff                                                                                  |
| Field Medic       | A direct heal, including a Forge ally buff with a heal attached                              |
| Warlock's Hex     | A damaging field, including a Forge projectile that places one on impact                     |
| Wind Step         | Dash                                                                                         |
| Borrowed Anthem   | An ally buff with the damage axis for Rousing Chorus or the protection axis for Warding Hymn |

A healing zone never receives the direct-heal Field Medic upgrade. An ally
buff called Hex never receives a damaging field grand. The bank keeps earned
grands through incompatible replacements and reclaiming. The eligible
receiving skill must be active to equip them.

Forge graft grand previews show the replacement's resolved stats rather than
the fixed donor description. Forge blink and dash descriptions now include
their actual immunity duration, so a defensive boost remains visible when
recharge is already at its floor.

## Identity choices

- **Ancestral Relay** stays eligible with a burst that can relay, or with a
  field that can spend Momentum and place Spirit Shelter away from the caster.
  Earth Totem remains a working option when Whirlwind is displaced and the kit
  can still build Momentum. A generic grafted field cannot substitute for it.
- **Grand Tactician** needs a damaging special that actually receives its
  steering action. A bound Precision Volley can supply it after Shield Bash
  is displaced. Rally alone cannot. Infernal Claim's detonation bypasses the
  direct steering route and no longer advertises one.

All fourteen native graft grands retained an effect. Across 40 real Forge
seeds, each grand had compatible and incompatible receivers. The checks
covered 560 combinations and verified that incompatible replay leaves the
ability unchanged. Focused fixtures exercised direct heals, healing fields,
impact fields, blink, dash and ally buff axes. The 4,458 fixed encounters
reproduced the existing results. This wraps up the current reward-choice pass.
