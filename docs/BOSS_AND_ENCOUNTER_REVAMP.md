# Boss and encounter revamp

Encounter production follows the shared visual pilot. Each encounter slice
designs its mechanics and visual presentation together, using the shared
language for shapes, motion, impacts, danger zones and combat states. This plan covers
bosses, summons and encounter rules as parts of the same fight.

1. **Audit the roster by player decisions.** Record how players read attacks,
   reposition, interrupt or protect allies, recover from mistakes, and react
   to phase transitions. Identify encounters that repeat a decision with
   different numbers and encounters where attack overlap makes decisions
   unreadable. Include arena terrain, cover, summons, flight and party size.
2. **Prototype one existing encounter.** Ancient Dragon is a candidate for
   testing flight, breath angles, knockback and re-entry. Keep its current
   version as a comparison. Test tells and safe routes with melee and ranged
   kits, solo and co-op, on desktop and phone. Then consider new mechanics
   such as changing routes between phases or coordinating roles to open a
   recovery window, based on where the prototype lacks decisions.
3. **Check encounter combinations.** Replay a seeded multi-boss fight with
   affixes or corruption. Evaluate concurrent tells, overlapping hazards,
   available escape paths and what players can reasonably read and act on.
   Use class mobility, control, recovery and protection as meaningful options
   while keeping multiple valid responses available.
4. **Expand in cohorts.** Start with bosses that have the least distinct
   decisions or poorest tell readability. Use playtest feedback and matched
   runs to check whether revisions improve fight variety and fairness before
   extending them to the rest of the roster.

Prototype with readable effects and representative silhouettes first. Finish
boss models, summons, arena art and attack effects once phases, safe routes
and hit geometry hold up in playtesting. Test multi-boss and modifier overlap
before rolling the treatment out in cohorts. Resume whole-game tuning once the
revised fights provide stable targets. See the [development roadmap](DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md).
